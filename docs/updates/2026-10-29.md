# 29 Oct 2026: Seller sets the cost, marketplace sets the price

**Short version:** a seller now enters only what they want to be paid per item (the **cost**). The marketplace (admin) decides the customer price and puts the product on sale.

**Who has to act:** the seller app team (breaking change). The customer app needs one small change.

---

## Seller app

| Before | Now |
|---|---|
| Seller sent `price` (required) | Seller sends `cost_price` (required, on every variant too) |
| Seller could make a product `active` | `active` now leaves it `pending`, waiting for the admin to price it |
| Earning = price − commission % | Earning = `cost_price × quantity` |

- `price` and `compare_at_price` still come back, but they are **read-only**. They read `0` until the admin prices the product.
- If the cost of a live product changes, it goes back to `pending` until it is priced again.
- New status `pending` means "awaiting price".
- New notification `product-went-live` is sent when the admin prices and publishes a product.

**What the app should do:**

- Replace the price field with a required "Cost per item".
- Show the customer price as text, or "Not priced yet" when it is `0`.
- Rename the publish button to "Send for pricing".
- Warn the seller before they change the cost of a live product.

## Customer app

- `POST /orders` now refuses a product that has come off sale. You get a `422` on `items` with the message "… is no longer available. Remove it to continue." Show the message and take the shopper back to the cart.
- Nothing else changes shape.

## Payouts (fix)

A payout's `net_amount` is now the sum of the sellers' actual earnings. Before, the seller also got the money from the marketplace's coupon by mistake.
